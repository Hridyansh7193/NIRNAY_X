Introduction & Background
The Indian legal ecosystem processes millions of documents annually — contracts, FIRs, judgments, petitions, and court notices. Citizens and
professionals alike face compounding challenges in navigating this volume with limited accessible, intelligent tooling.
An immense volume of highly specialized legal documents with
complex terminology creates significant barriers to comprehension
for non-specialists and professionals alike.
Recent advances in Natural Language Processing (NLP), Large Language
Models (LLMs), and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) have created
a genuine opportunity to build reliable, context-aware legal assistance
tools.
Conventional keyword-based search systems fail to capture semantic
relationships between legal concepts, producing incomplete or
misleading retrieval results.
Citizens struggle to understand legal documents; legal professionals
spend disproportionate time on manual research rather than 
strategic advocacy and client service.
General-purpose Large Language Models generate plausible but
unsupported information without grounding in reliable external legal
sources — a critical liability in legal contexts.
Integrate AI-driven semantic search, document processing, and RAG into
a unified legal intelligence platform that is grounded, reliable, and
accessible to both citizens and professionals.

System Architecture
Nirnay-X operates as a modular end-to-end pipeline, transforming raw legal PDFs into grounded, context-aware responses — with supporting modules for
case workflow management.
Each stage is modular — enabling independent component upgrades (e.g.,
embedding model, LLM, vector store) without full pipeline reconstruction.
Visual organization of chronological case events, procedural
milestones, and filing deadlines across active matters.
Semantic similarity search across case documents to surface
related precedents and analogous fact patterns automatically.
Structured calendar integration for scheduling hearings, tracking
court dates, and issuing deadline reminders.
Architecture separates ingestion, retrieval, and generation into
distinct layers — ensuring traceability of sources in every
generated response.

=== SLIDE/PAGE 7 ===
RAG Workflow: Step-by-Step
The Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline is the technical core of Nirnay-X. Each of the eleven sequential steps is designed to ensure that every LLM
response is grounded in retrieved, verifiable legal content — eliminating unsupported generation.
User uploads a PDF — contract, judgment, FIR, notice, or petition
The user's natural-language question is embedded using the same model
OCR and text extraction modules process the document for downstream use
Text is divided into meaningful, legally coherent segments for granular retrieval
Embeddings are indexed alongside metadata for efficient similarity-based lookup
The most semantically relevant legal passages are selected as retrieval candidates
Response and source information are presented together, enabling user verification
Each chunk is converted into a dense vector representation capturing semantic meaning
Query vector is compared against stored embeddings to find contextually similar chunks
LLM produces a context-aware, grounded answer based exclusively on retrieved information
Retrieved chunks are supplied as grounding context — constraining the LLM to sourced content
